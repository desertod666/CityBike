CURRENT ESP32 READER SOURCE: 0.6.7

Adds one fixed 100 us GPIO4 LOW hold / GPIO5 readback to the isolated USB
selftest, between pad self-echo and the external transceiver CAN echo stage.
TWAI must be uninstalled. The test requires bike CAN physically detached;
GPIO4 returns HIGH before leaving its critical section. Failed level readback
prevents the external CAN echo stage. It does not identify a faulty component.
The stage3 latest observed ECC event is saved before inhibition/STOP, so
cleanup-induced error flags cannot overwrite the diagnostic snapshot.

The portal build is 20261030-gpioprobe1. Normal CAN timing, modes, battery
request policy and BLE controls are unchanged. No arbitrary transmit endpoint.
Prefer GitHub-hosted reader-ci with pinned tools. Verify source hashes,
native tests, exact ELF assets and image size before publication.
